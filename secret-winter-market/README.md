# The Secret Winter Market

60-second vertical Short (1080×1920, 30 fps) in the 2026 winter house style. Underground market, warm lantern glow, no words except the breath count, no audio.

The main lady ("you") has dark medium-length hair, a warm smile, a green coat and a brown scarf.
The green coat is a one-off outside the house palette, approved for this video.
