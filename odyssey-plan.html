A five-year life & career sketch

Odyssey Plan

Sketch three plausible five-year versions of your life side by side, then rate each one honestly. The point isn't to pick a winner — it's to see options you didn't know you had. Adapted from the Odyssey Plan exercise in Designing Your Life by Bill Burnett & Dave Evans.

How to use this: name each plan, sketch what happens year by year, then rate it on the dashboard below. Everything you type is saved automatically in this browser.
