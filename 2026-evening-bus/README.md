# Evening bus: "The dark isn't empty"

A 45s vertical Short (1080×1920, 30fps) in the 2026 winter animation house style. Night throughout, one counted breath, no words except the breath count, no audio.

| Time | Shot |
| --- | --- |
| 0–6.8s | A night street. The bus glides in, then a slow push towards the lady's window |
| 6–20s | Inside: the lady by the window. Breath 1-2-3 in (11–14s), 1-2-3 out (14–17s), steam on the glass, the steam fades (17–20s) |
| 19.6–33s | Through the window: the baker kneading dough, then a dad reading to a sleepy child |
| 32–37.8s | The nurse resting her head on the bus window |
| 37–45s | Pull back: rows of windows, most lit and a few dark. One dark window slowly lights up during the pause. Fade to cream |
